## Glossary

- clue — one short English line that leads to a word in the sense of its translation, shown with the word itself (and its -s/-es/-ed/-ing forms) as "___". A word has three graded clues: clue 1 vague, clue 2 closer, clue 3 almost a giveaway. NOT a definition card of match-definitions (clue 3 may be the same text, but clues 1 and 2 never are).
- grid — the round's words (3 to 9) shown as tappable English words in a shuffled order, three per row. NOT a fixed 3×3 board and NOT the group (only the round's words are in it).
- target — the word the shown clues lead to; each word of a round is the target once, in random order. NOT the selected word (a tap on the grid is a guess, not a target).
- found / greyed — a found word was guessed (or revealed) as a target and stays marked and inert for the rest of the round; a greyed word was a wrong guess for the current target only and comes back for the next target. NOT a word result (nothing is saved).
- usable word — here: a word to learn of the selected group with a non-empty word, translation and row id, not a repeat of an earlier word, whose three shown clues each hold a letter or digit besides "___". NOT the usable word of other exercises.
