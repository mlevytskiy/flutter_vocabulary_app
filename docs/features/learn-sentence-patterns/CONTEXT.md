## Glossary

- pattern card — the card of one word: a model sentence with the word in bold, the line "Pattern: …", the line "Use it to: …", the translation and the speaker. NOT the learner's sentence (that is typed under the card after "My turn").
- pattern — the reusable frame of the model sentence, at most 60 characters, with the changeable parts written as slots ("[noun]", "[verb]", "[adjective]", "[sentence]", "[verb in Past Simple]") and without the word itself. NOT a grammar rule name (it is the frame to copy, not an explanation).
- sentence check — one AI check of one learner sentence for one word and task (pattern, own, translate, explain), decided finally by the app's rule. NOT a typed-word match (a single typed word is matched by the app alone, without AI).
- check outcome — how a word ends in a sentence exercise: right, fixed, skipped (Skip before any check was sent) or not checked (Skip after a sent check failed). NOT a word result (only an exam turns an outcome into known / repeat).
- usable word — here: a word to learn of the selected group that has a translation and a Worker pattern whose sentence holds the word once. NOT the usable word of other exercises.
