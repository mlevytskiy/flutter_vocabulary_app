## Glossary

- gap sentence — one English sentence that holds the word exactly once, in exactly its stored form, taken from a linked film dialogue line (cut to that one sentence) or, failing that, from the Worker. NOT the example line of remember-or-not (that one is a whole line and may hold another form of the word).
- gap — the one "___" that stands for the word inside the gap sentence; a phrase ("give up") is one gap. NOT a blank answer field.
- gap question — a gap sentence with its gap, the word's translation as the cue under it, and the word as the expected answer; the same question serves practice (fill-the-gaps) and the Quiz exam. NOT a choice question (no options).
- cue — the word's translation shown under the gap sentence, with the English word masked if it appears there. NOT the first-letter hint (that one shows only after a wrong first try, in practice).
- try — one sent, non-empty answer to a gap question; practice allows two, an exam one. NOT a "Show answer" tap (that ends the word without a try).
- usable word — here: a word to learn of the selected group that has a translation and a gap sentence. NOT the usable word of other exercises.
