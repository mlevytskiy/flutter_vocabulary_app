## Glossary

- match board — the shared two-column round of the match exercises: words on the left in group order, answers (synonyms here, definitions in match-definitions) on the right, shuffled; the learner links each word to its answer by two taps. NOT the shared page (nothing here is played on the web).
- pair — one word together with its answer on the match board; a right pair locks and stays linked for the rest of the round. NOT a word row (a pair exists only inside one round).
- miss — one wrong pair tried by the learner, counted on the word of that pair; repeated wrong tries each count. NOT "Missed" in pick-the-answer (there a word is either right or missed once).
- synonym — one English word or short phrase that the Worker gives for a word in the sense of its translation, used as that word's answer on the board. NOT a translation (a synonym is English).
- usable word — here: a word to learn of the selected group that has a synonym and survives the clash rule (no two cards of one board that read the same, no second right answer). NOT the usable word of pick-the-answer (that one depends on four options, not on a synonym).
