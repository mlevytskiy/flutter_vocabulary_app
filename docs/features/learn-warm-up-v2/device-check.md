# Device check — learn-warm-up-v2

Do this on the phone after the Worker is deployed (deploy the Worker before the app).

## First warm-up (AC-01, AC-02, AC-06)
- [ ] Clear the level in Learn settings, open Learn: the warm-up opens with "What's your English level?" and six taps (Beginner, Elementary, Intermediate, Upper-intermediate, Advanced, Not sure).
- [ ] After a tap the AI reacts in one short line, then the main question follows.
- [ ] The step bar shows 3 or 4 steps, the gear is there, there is no Skip, the mood is not saved.
- [ ] Open Learn again: the intro is not asked (also after "Not sure").

## Questions and own words (AC-03, AC-04, AC-05)
- [ ] Questions come in random order in rounds of three; an unanswered one stays first next time.
- [ ] "Something else" opens own words; Send is disabled when empty or over 80 characters, typed and spoken.
- [ ] Each answer gets one short reaction line; with the network off a fixed line shows within about 2 s.

## Grammar (AC-07, AC-08)
- [ ] On every 5th warm-up a grammar question appears (never together with the intro); a rule of your level is offered.
- [ ] After the talk the results page shows the grammar line (right/wrong counts only).

## Topics (AC-13, AC-14, AC-15)
- [ ] The topic step shows 3 topics and "My own topic…", each with a one-line situation of at most 20 words.
- [ ] At most one topic comes from the profile, and only in the third place.
- [ ] With the network off the 3 bundled topics show after about 4 s.
- [ ] The new topics (Meeting new people, Helping customers, ...) can be picked and the talk runs.

## Results page (AC-10, AC-16)
- [ ] On the 3rd, 6th, 9th talk the page asks "Was this talk interesting?" with thumbs up and down; one tap records it and hides the question.
- [ ] The story page after the cards must stay put when the screen turns to landscape, and the rating buttons must work afterwards.

## Profile in settings (AC-11, AC-12)
- [ ] Learn settings shows level, goal and daily context (or "Not set"), each can be changed or cleared, under the note "Used only to pick talk topics".
- [ ] A cleared level brings the intro back on the next warm-up.
- [ ] Share a collection: the shared page shows no profile.
