## Glossary

- exam — a Step 3 exercise: one locked answer per word, no hint, no retry, no right-or-wrong before the summary, and a word result saved for every word that ends. NOT a practice exercise (Steps 1–2 show feedback at once and save nothing).
- word result — the label an exam gives one word, "known" or "repeat", saved on the session with the exam and the time; the newest one per word wins. NOT a score (no numbers) and NOT part of the word row (never shared, published or exported).
- known — the word result of a word answered right on its only answer (a one-letter slip counts as right). NOT "seen" (a word that was shown but never answered has no result).
- repeat — the word result of a word answered wrong, skipped, or (in a sentence exam) corrected. NOT "not checked" (a check that never answered gives no result at all).
- choice question — a question with four options, where the first tap is the answer: kind A shows the word and offers four translations, kind B shows the meaning and offers four English words. NOT a gap question (typed answer).
- quiz attempt — one run of the Quiz from question 1 to its summary: either the first attempt after Start, or a Repeat attempt over the words labelled repeat. NOT a round (the Quiz has no rounds; every attempt is one sequence of at most 7 questions).
- usable word — here: a word to learn of the selected group, with a translation, for which a choice question of both kinds can be built, or a gap question exists, or both. NOT the usable word of other exercises.
