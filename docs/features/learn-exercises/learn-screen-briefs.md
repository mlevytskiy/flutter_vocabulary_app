# Learn screen: a brief idea for each item

The Learn screen after the plan: 15 items in 3 steps. Status: built, planned, or blocked
(speech-to-text, owner). Subtitles are what the learner sees (60 characters or fewer).
"Speak" is a mode inside Make your own sentences and Translate sentences, not a separate item.

## Step 1: passive (7 items)
Goal: words go into the passive vocabulary. The learner recognises them.

### Mnemonic story (built)
Subtitle: A short story with a picture to hook the words
You read an AI-written story that uses the group's words, with one picture. A story with
images is easier to remember than a list. You get a story and a picture for the group, kept in story runs.

### Dialogs from movie (built)
Subtitle: Learn the words inside real film scenes
You read a scene from the film the words came from, with a scene picture. Words stay tied to a
moment you know. You get the scene with its words in bold. Needs dialogues from subtitles.

### Match synonyms (planned)
Subtitle: Link each word to a word with the same meaning
You tap a word, then its synonym, in two columns. Neighbouring words show what the word covers.
You get a list of your misses per word.

### Match antonyms (planned)
Subtitle: Link each word to its opposite
Same board as synonyms, but you link opposites. Contrast fixes the meaning. Words with no real
opposite are left out. You get a list of your misses per word.

### Match word and definition (planned)
Subtitle: Link each word to its English definition
You link words to short English definitions, with the word hidden as "___". You think in English,
not through your language. You get a list of your misses per word.

### Guess my word (planned)
Subtitle: Find the word from three clues
You see a clue and tap the word in a grid. A wrong tap greys the word and shows a closer clue.
Each new clue is a new look at the word. You get a score per word: 3, 2, 1 or 0.

### Pick the right answer (planned)
Subtitle: Choose the right option out of four
You get a word with four translations, or a meaning with four words. Choosing is the easiest
test of recognition. You get a quick right or wrong on each question.

## Step 2: active (4 items)
Goal: words go into the active vocabulary. The learner produces them.

### Fill the gaps (planned)
Subtitle: Type the missing word in a sentence
You see a sentence with a gap and its translation, and type the word. You must produce the word,
not just know it. A near miss helps with the first letter. You get a result per word: 1st try, 2nd try, missed.

### Remember or not (planned)
Subtitle: Flip the card and say if you remembered
You see a meaning, try to recall the word, flip the card, and rate yourself. Honest recall is
the work. Missed cards come back once. You get a list of what you remembered. Includes the old flashcards.

### Sentence patterns (planned)
Subtitle: Reuse a ready pattern to write your own sentence
You see a good sentence with a pattern and when to use it, then write your own with the same
word. A pattern makes the first sentences easy. You get an AI check and a fix for each sentence.

### Chain story (planned)
Subtitle: Continue the story, one word at a time
You take the group's mnemonic story and add one sentence per word, in English. You use words
in context, in a plot you know. You get a story you built together with the AI. Needs the group's story first.

## Step 3: exam (4 items)
Goal: examine the learner. Each word gets a result: known well, or needs repeating.
These exercises have no hints and no second try. The saved result needs one owner decision first.

### Make your own sentences (planned)
Subtitle: Write a sentence with each word, no help
You see only the word and write one sentence. If you can use it alone, you know it. You get
known or repeat per word, with the AI's fix. Includes the old writing practice. Has a Speak mode (blocked: speech-to-text).

### Translate sentences (planned)
Subtitle: Translate a sentence into English with the word
You see a sentence in your language and write it in English with the target word. It tests
the word from meaning alone. You get known or repeat per word and the model translation. Has a Speak mode (blocked: speech-to-text).

### Explain it to a friend (planned)
Subtitle: Explain the word without saying it
You explain the word in English, typed in 90 s or aloud to a friend who says "Understood".
Explaining shows real control of a word. You get known or repeat per word.

### Quiz (planned)
Subtitle: A short mixed test: choices and gaps
You answer up to 7 mixed questions, choices and gaps, with no feedback until the end. A mix
gives a fair mark. You get "N of n known", the right answers for misses, and a Repeat for them.
