## Glossary

- own sentence — the one English sentence the learner types for a word with nothing shown but the word; checked once in the sense of the word's stored translation. NOT a sentence copied from a pattern (sentence-patterns) or a translation of a given sentence (translate-sentences).
- usable word — here: a word to learn of the selected group with a non-empty word, translation and row id; no Worker data is needed. NOT the usable word of other exercises.
