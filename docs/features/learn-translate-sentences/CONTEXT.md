## Glossary

- source sentence — the Ukrainian sentence of one word's card, made by the Worker for the word in the sense of its translation; it never contains an English word of the group. NOT the learner's translation (that is typed under the card).
- cue — the exact part of the source sentence that stands for the word, shown in bold so the learner knows which English word is meant. NOT the stored translation (the cue may be an inflected form of it).
- model translation — the Worker's English translation of the source sentence, holding the word exactly once; shown, with the word in bold, only after the word is final. NOT the corrected sentence (that keeps the learner's own wording).
- usable word — here: a word to learn of the selected group with a translation and row id whose Worker data is fair: a source sentence without any English word of the group, a cue found in it, and a model translation holding the word once. NOT the usable word of other exercises.
